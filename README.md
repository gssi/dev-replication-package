# Replication Package

Materials for **“Investigating Developers’ Soft-Ethical Preferences in the Age of LLM-Based Coding Assistants.”** The paper is currently under review and will be available upon acceptance.

The study investigates developers’ agency over implementation choices left open by requirements and constraints, and how to elicit the soft-ethical preferences behind those choices. It combines a survey of 104 practitioners, a multidisciplinary focus group, expert review, and developer piloting. It identifies six aspects: fairness, inclusiveness, privacy, transparency and explainability, traceability, and resource consumption.

## Repository contents

- **[RQ1 — Developer agency](rq1/):** anonymized survey responses, analysis notebooks, and participant background charts.
- **[RQ2 — Soft-ethical aspects](rq2/):** focus group slides, transcript, and aspect evolution.
- **[RQ3 — Preference elicitation](rq3/):** aspect definitions, scenario-based questionnaire items, instrument description, expert interviews, and an interactive questionnaire and profile comparison tool.
